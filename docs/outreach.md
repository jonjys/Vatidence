**Subject:** VIES consultation numbers without the 40-second-per-number grind

yes/no from VIES — it is the consultation number that records who checked which
VAT, and when. VIES only issues that number if the requester sends their own
VAT number. Most people skip that step on the Commission site. That identifier
is what VIES returned, not a legal opinion.

## Who to send this to (and who not to)

**Worth one message:** Swedish/EU accounting firms, freelance bookkeepers,
and export-finance people who already mention intra-EU sales, reverse charge,
or VIES on their site. Prefer a named person.

**Not worth it:** purchased lists, “VAT database” operators, anyone who would
need this product explained from zero, cold blasts, or follow-ups past one
polite ping.

If they reply “we already do this on the Commission site”, the only useful
sentence is: the consultation number is missing unless they typed their own
VAT every time.
